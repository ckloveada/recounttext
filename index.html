1 Hakikat & Struktur Personal Recount Text

Pendalaman materi teoritis recount text dalam konteks pertandingan olahraga internasional.

Materi Kelas X SMK

Hakikat & Purpose (Tujuan)

Chronological Personal Experience

Personal Recount Text adalah teks yang menceritakan kembali peristiwa atau pengalaman masa lalu yang dialami sendiri oleh penulis secara berurutan (chronological order).

  • To Inform: Memberikan laporan faktual tentang momen pertandingan.
  • To Entertain: Mengajak pembaca merasakan euforia kemenangan atau ketegangan match.

Perbedaan dengan Descriptive Text

Statis vs Dinamis

Descriptive Text (Bab 1) - STATIS

Fokus pada gambaran wujud, ciri-ciri, dan karakter fisik atlet.

Example: "Ragnar Oratmangoen is an agile striker with sharp instincts."

Personal Recount Text (Bab 2) - DINAMIS

Fokus pada urutan peristiwa dari awal hingga akhir pertandingan.

Example: "Last Friday, I watched Ragnar score a brilliant goal in the 88th minute..."

Generic Structure Breakdown

Part 1
1. Orientation

Mengenalkan latar belakang (Who, Where, When, What).

"Last Sunday, my friends and I watched the FIFA ASEAN Cup match between Indonesia and Vietnam live at Gelora Bung Karno Stadium."
Part 2
2. Series of Events

Urutan kejadian kronologis selama pertandingan.

"First, both teams started with high intensity. In the second half, Marselino passed the ball smoothly and scored a golden goal..."
Part 3
3. Re-orientation

Kesimpulan, perasaan pribadi, & evaluasi akhir.

"Even though I was completely exhausted, I felt extremely proud to witness Team Indonesia's historical victory!"

Sebagai siswa SMK yang berkarakter, menyampaikan recount text juga merefleksikan nilai-nilai etika suporter saat menyaksikan ajang FIFA ASEAN Cup atau Asian Games 2026:

Positive Behaviors (Harus Ditiru)
  • Menghargai lagu kebangsaan & bendera lawan.
  • Mendukung secara kreatif (chanting, koreografi banner).
  • Melakukan Clean-Up Drive (membuang sampah sendiri di tribune).
  • Apresiasi perjuangan tim baik menang maupun kalah.
Negative Behaviors (Wajib Dihindari)
  • Melakukan Hooliganism atau provokasi fisik & verbal.
  • Ujaran kebencian/rasialisme di stadion maupun media sosial.
  • Melempar flare, botol, atau merusak fasilitas umum.

2 Five Senses (Sensory Details) Matrix

Eksplorasi kata sensorik untuk membuat cerita Recount hidup (*Vivid Storytelling*).

1. Sight (Penglihatan)

Visual suasana pertandingan

Red-and-white scarves Bright LED lights Green turf field Tense expressions

2. Sound (Pendengaran)

Audio & keramaian stadion

Deafening whistles Roaring chants Thumping drums Squeaking shoes

3. Touch (Sentuhan)

Sensasi fisik & cuaca

Sticky sweat Cold evening breeze Warm high-fives Goosebumps

4. Smell (Penciuman)

Aroma sekitar venue

Fresh cut grass Roasted corn aroma Smoky atmosphere Street food smell

5. Taste (Pengecapan)

Rasa camilan & minuman

Ice-cold mineral water Salty butter popcorn Sweet iced tea Savory meatballs

Time Connectives

Penanda urutan waktu wajib dalam Recount Text:

First / Initially Awal kejadian
Then / After that Kejadian berikutnya
Suddenly / Meanwhile Momen krusial
Finally / In the end Penutup pertandingan
Tip: Gunakan variasi connectives agar paragraf tidak monoton!

Section A

Reading Comprehension & Text Feature Extraction

Passage: Thrilling Victory at Asian Games 2026

Last night, I sat in front of the television to watch the Asian Games 2026 badminton semifinals. At first, the room was quiet as Alwi Farhan entered the court. Suddenly, the crowd in the arena roared loudly as he delivered a powerful jump smash. My palms felt sweaty from the intense rally. Finally, when he secured the winning point, I jumped off my seat and cheered enthusiastically.

4. Which of the following is a POSITIVE behavior for sports spectators?
